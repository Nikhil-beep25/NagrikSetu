## 🖥️ Slide 2: The Problem
* **Slide Title:** **The Civic Friction: Why Citizen Complaints Fail**
* **Visual Concept:** A 3-column split showing the pain points experienced by a resident on a street.
* **Key Points:**
  1. **Jurisdictional Maze:** Citizens don't know which authority owns their road—Municipal Corporation, Development Authority, PWD, or Utility Board.
  2. **Articulation Gap:** Informal or colloquial complaints are often rejected by administrative desks for missing landmarks, statutory classifications, or formal tone.
  3. **Safety Blindspots:** Acute life-threatening hazards (e.g., exposed sparking wires) get queued alongside routine 15-day paperwork tickets.
  4. **The Intermediary Trap:** Applying for state certificates (Income, Domicile) is obscured by complex requirements, driving citizens to unauthorized touts.
* **Presenter Cue:**  
  *"The issue in Indian cities isn't a lack of civic willingness; it’s that citizen reports are filed to the wrong department, in incomplete formats, with zero safety triage."*

---

## 🖥️ Slide 3: Our Solution
* **Slide Title:** **NagrikSetu: An Intelligent Citizen Bridge**
* **Visual Concept:** Diagram showing an arrow from *"Citizen in Hindi/Hinglish"* through *"NagrikSetu Intelligence Layer"* to *"Exact Government Department / Portal"*.
* **Key Pillars:**
  1. **Natural Colloquial Input:** Voice or text in English, Hindi, and Hinglish.
  2. **Automated Administrative Structuring:** Converts messy descriptions into standard Citizen Charter formal grievance letters.
  3. **Intelligent Authority Matching:** Maps jurisdiction accurately across municipal, development, and state utility bodies.
  4. **Pre-Flight Emergency Interceptor:** Prioritizes physical danger first, routing citizens to 112/1912 within seconds.
  5. **Verified Service Navigation:** Clear document checklists and direct `.gov.in` portal links.
* **Presenter Cue:**  
  *"NagrikSetu acts as the citizen-first triage layer that sits before complex government backends, ensuring every voice is properly formatted, routed, and heard."*

## 🖥️ Slide 4: How NagrikSetu Works
* **Slide Title:** **The 4-Step Citizen Journey**
* **Visual Concept:** Linear progressive step cards with clean iconography.
* **Steps:**
  1. **Step 1: Express (व्यक्त करें):** The citizen describes the problem via speech or text in everyday words.
  2. **Step 2: Understand & Triage (समझें):** Gemini AI extracts category, severity, landmarks, and assesses immediate physical risk.
  3. **Step 3: Route & Structure (मार्गदर्शन):** Generates a formal representation letter and recommends the verified local authority (e.g., Agra Nagar Nigam).
  4. **Step 4: Act & Track (समाधान):** Citizen tracks grievance progress on a personal ledger or opens the official government portal directly.
* **Presenter Cue:**  
  *"From a messy Hinglish voice note to an official petition addressed to the Municipal Commissioner in under 3 seconds."*

## 🖥️ Slide 6: Technology Architecture
* **Slide Title:** **Resilient Full-Stack GovTech Architecture**
* **Visual Concept:** Architectural tier stack: React 18 Frontend ↔ Express Node.js Gateway ↔ Dual Engine (Gemini AI + Deterministic Local NLP).
* **Technical Highlights:**
  * **Frontend:** React 18 + Vite, Tailwind CSS, Oxlint (0 errors), responsive on all mobile viewports.
  * **Backend Gateway:** Node.js Express API, sliding-window rate limiting (40 req/min/IP), origin CORS protection.
  * **Dual-Engine Resilience:** Google Gemini AI (3.5/2.5 Flash) backed by a 100% deterministic local NLP engine for instant offline fallback.
  * **Verified Quality:** 11 out of 11 automated test suites passing with 100% verification.
* **Presenter Cue:**  
  *"We built NagrikSetu with enterprise-grade resilience. If cloud networks drop or API limits are hit, our deterministic local engine steps in with zero disruption."*
